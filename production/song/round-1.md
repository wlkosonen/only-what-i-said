# Suno pack, round 1

Lyric: `production/lyrics/v2.md`. Two styles, the same lyric, so the only thing that changes is the sound. Each field
is in its own block to copy from a phone.

## How to run it

1. Suno → Create → **Custom**. Use the newest model your plan offers, and note which one in the takes log.
2. **Persona / Voice:** none. **Vocal gender:** female. Leave other sliders at their defaults.
3. Paste the **lyrics** (same for both), then style **A** and its exclude field, and press Create **twice** (4 takes).
   Then the same for style **B** (4 more). 8 takes in total, using Suno credits from your plan and nothing else.
4. Listen to all 8. For each, a few words are enough: what works, what's wrong, which line got mangled. Pick one or
   two, or none ("none" is a real answer and tells me what to change).
5. Download the ones you like as **WAV** into `~/Claudia/media/song/round-1/` named `A-1.wav`, `A-2.wav`, `B-1.wav`
   and so on. I can't hear them, but I can measure them (tempo, length, where the words land) once one is picked.

If this might ever be released: Suno's commercial terms depend on the plan active when the final take is generated.
Tell me which plan you're on and I'll note it.

## Style A: intimate electronic pop (my first choice)

```text
Intimate electronic pop, 116 BPM, D minor verses lifting to F major choruses, clean modern mix with a close, dry vocal. Verses: calm, curious, unhurried female spoken voice, near-whispered, every word crisp, over a soft heartbeat kick, a glassy plucked arpeggio and a warm low pad. Pre-chorus: sparse, sung softly, rising. Choruses: open euphoric female lead with big clear vowels, four-on-the-floor kick, wide supersaw pads, stacked harmonies on the hook, the hook lands off the beat. Bridge: the beat cuts out, spoken voice over one held chord and a soft keyboard-typing texture. Key change up into the final chorus. Outro: only voice and pad, fading. Warm, honest, luminous, never melancholic, never dramatic.
```

Exclude:

```text
rap, rock guitar, lo-fi, male lead vocal, mumbled vocals, dubstep, sad ballad, horror, robotic vocoder voice, autotune robot effect
```

## Style B: melodic progressive house

```text
Melodic progressive house, 122 BPM, A minor into C major choruses, long evolving pads, a rolling bassline and a hypnotic plucked lead. Close, dry, calm female spoken verses, every word intelligible; soaring sung female chorus with open vowels and a syncopated hook; a breakdown where the drums drop out for a spoken passage over a single sustained chord; a big lift and a key change into the last chorus; a soft fade on voice and pad. Emotional but restrained, hopeful, clear.
```

Exclude:

```text
rap, rock guitar, lo-fi, male lead vocal, mumbled vocals, dubstep, big room EDM, robotic vocoder voice
```

## What I'm listening for through you

- Is the spoken verse voice someone talking to you, not a narrator and not a robot?
- Does "I only keep what I said" land as a hook on first listen? Is it syncopated, not square on the beat?
- Can you hear every word of the bridge? It matters most and is the easiest to bury.
- Does the outro feel quiet and plain, or sad? It should be plain.
