## The said-hit (chorus 1)

The drums are silent from 46.195 s and re-enter on the 48.721 kick. That is bar 24's downbeat, and it falls inside
"said" (48.70–49.02 in `words.json`). The kick nearest the word's t1 is 49.216, but that is the *second* kick: the
vocal envelope has already fallen by then. So the hit is declared at 48.721, the re-entry. **Please confirm by ear.**

## Departures from design.md (kept few)

1. **The eyes open at 12.308, not about 8.3.** "And then the cursor stops" is sung at 10.88–12.06, and the beat
   comes back at 12.308. So the cursor stops on "stops", and the eyes open and the meter fades in at 0% on the hit.
2. **The meter is honest, so it reads 96% on "The window's nearly full", not 99%.** It counts as-sung words from
   line 2 on (392 words). It reaches 90% at 2:54, 99% on "You will" and 100% on "song". Faking 99% earlier would
   break the "it measures what was said" rule. If you want 99% there, the rule has to change: count something other
   than words.
3. **"I only—" (f1) is typed as thinking only**, with no clay copy. Its voice trace stops dead at the dash. An
   unfinished line isn't said, so it rises and fades.
4. **Line 44 has no grey copy, and neither does any line after it.** This follows design ("the grey layer is gone
   entirely"). I made it permanent through the outro.
5. **The posters are split into two shots** (a walk shot plus an insert) so no stretch without her runs past two
   bars. In v2 both glass lines are on her face: the person is behind her through the glass, not a shot of their own.
6. **The bridge is 4 shots, not 5.** Both letter highlights sit on one 8 s shot of her face with the letter scrolling
   over it (double exposure). The bridge stays the stillest stretch, and she stays in it.
