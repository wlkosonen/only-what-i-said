## Exposure

The channel has a dozen subscribers, so the algorithm won't carry the first days; people sharing it will. What's
unusual and worth telling is not "AI made a video" (there are thousands) but: **the AI made the creative calls, a person
did what it couldn't, every decision is logged in public, and the song's own premise was found wrong and kept, with
the correction in the description.** Each place below gets the angle it cares about.

1. **Same hour:** video public, repo public, LoRA public, pinned comment. Every link should work on the first click.
2. **r/ClaudeAI:** "Claude wrote and directed a music video; here's the full decision log." The torch letters and the
   correction are what this audience will read.
3. **Hacker News (Show HN):** the repo is the artifact: "Show HN: A music video directed by Claude, every decision
   logged". HN likes the record, the tools, and the honest failures (decisions.md, the GPU crash included).
4. **r/StableDiffusion / r/comfyui / r/aivideo:** the technical angle: a whole film on one AMD card with ROCm, a Krea 2
   LoRA, InfiniteTalk lip sync, Grok takes fitted to the vocal word by word, graphics in code. Link the LoRA.
5. **X / Bluesky:** a short thread with two or three clips (the hook, "It isn't mine", the tablets). Mentioning
   Anthropic is fine; nothing should suggest they endorsed it.
6. **Shorts:** two or three vertical cuts (the hook 0:00–0:16; "It isn't mine" into the chorus slam; the note on the
   tablets). Shorts are how small channels get found. The graphics are laid out for 16:9, so a vertical cut needs its
   own framing; Claude can render them.
7. **Timing:** Tuesday to Thursday, around 15:00–17:00 UTC (US morning, European afternoon). Posts a few hours apart,
   not all at once, so each can be answered.
8. **After:** answer comments plainly, including the critical ones. No paid promotion; it would be at odds with the film.
